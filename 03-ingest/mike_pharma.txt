Michael Monaco — Pharmaceutical Industry Experience
===================================================

Michael Monaco has deep experience in the pharmaceutical and life sciences industry
spanning his time at Instem (2022–present) and d-wise (2020–2022). All of his
consulting work at both companies was for pharmaceutical clients, CROs (Contract
Research Organizations), and life sciences organizations operating under FDA
compliance and GxP regulatory requirements.

INSTEM — Pharmaceutical Cloud Consulting (2022–present)
-------------------------------------------------------
Instem is a pharmaceutical and life sciences software and services company.
Every client Mike works with at Instem is a pharma company or CRO.

Mike consulted at over two dozen pharmaceutical and CRO clients during his tenure
at Instem, ranging from small boutique organizations to some of the largest
pharmaceutical companies and contract research organizations in the world.
Deployment sizes ranged from 6-user pilot environments to enterprise rollouts
supporting over 2,000 users. Most clients adopted AWS, with some on Azure.

Key pharma cloud work at Instem:
- Architected AWS and Azure cloud migrations for SAS analytical applications in
  major pharmaceutical companies using lift-and-shift and cloud modernization
- Deployed and administered Posit Workbench, Posit Connect, and Posit Package
  Manager in regulated pharmaceutical environments supporting FDA-compliant
  analytics workloads
- Designed disaster recovery implementations with RTO of 1 day and RPO of 1 hour
- Implemented production/DR architectures across two AWS or Azure regions
- Designed storage solutions using NetApp ONTAP with SnapMirror replication,
  Nasuni, AWS Storage Gateway, Lustre FSX, and FSX for NetApp
- Developed Docker containers for SAS 9.4 integrated with AWS EKS
- Built Lambda-based cold start automation for SAS autoscaling clusters to
  control compute costs while maintaining availability
- Led Active Directory integrations for pharma clients: self-hosted AD, AWS
  Managed AD, Azure AD/Entra ID, SSSD, Centrify, Okta SAML/OIDC, GPOs
- Deployed infrastructure via Terraform and CloudFormation in CI/CD pipelines
- Managed a team of 5 cloud consultants serving pharma clients
