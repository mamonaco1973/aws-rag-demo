CURRENT ROLE — Instem, Manager of Cloud Consultants (November 2022 – present)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mike leads a team of 5 cloud consultants at Instem, a life sciences software and
services company. His primary focus is architecting and delivering AWS and Azure cloud
migrations for SAS analytical applications at pharmaceutical and CRO clients.

Over his tenure at Instem, Mike has consulted at over two dozen pharma and CRO clients,
ranging from small boutique organizations to some of the largest pharmaceutical companies
and contract research organizations in the world. Deployments ranged from 6-user pilot
environments to enterprise rollouts supporting over 2,000 users. Most clients adopted
AWS, with some adopting Azure.

The typical architecture was production in one AWS or Azure region with a failover
environment in a secondary region for disaster recovery. RTO targets were typically
one day; RPO targets were typically one hour. Some implementations used automated
failover — load balancers redirected by DNS changes, backed by autoscaling groups.
Others were manual failover procedures, typical of RDP-based SaaS delivery models.
For the data layer, NetApp with SnapMirror replication was the standard approach
across implementations.

Mike deployed and administered the full Posit data science platform stack — Posit
Workbench, Posit Connect, and Posit Package Manager — in regulated cloud environments
supporting pharmaceutical analytics workloads under FDA compliance requirements.

He designed storage solutions using Nasuni, AWS Storage Gateway, Lustre FSX, and
FSX for NetApp ONTAP. He developed Docker containers for running SAS 9.4 and
integrated them with AWS Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS).

A notable piece of engineering was a Lambda-based cold start solution for SAS
autoscaling clusters. The problem: to control compute costs, SAS clusters were scaled
down to zero nodes during idle periods. The Lambda function automatically triggered
the autoscaling group to bring the cluster back to one node, allowing SAS services
to restart without manual intervention. This gave clients cost savings without
sacrificing the ability to resume work quickly.

Mike led multiple Active Directory integration projects. His AD experience includes:
- Self-hosted Active Directory on EC2 and on-premises
- AWS Managed Microsoft AD
- Azure AD and Entra ID, including two-way trust configurations
- Linux host authentication using SSSD and Centrify
- SAML and OIDC federation, primarily with Okta
- Group Policy Objects (GPOs) for Linux and Windows hosts
- Single sign-on integrations for SAS and other analytical platforms

He deployed cloud infrastructure using Terraform and CloudFormation through CI/CD
pipelines in GitLab and GitHub, following DevOps principles in a highly regulated
pharmaceutical and FDA-compliance environment.

PREVIOUS ROLE — d-wise, Senior Cloud Consultant (March 2020 – November 2022)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
At d-wise, a pharmaceutical technology consulting firm, Mike supported and built
SAS Grid deployments for pharma clients and provided Linux administration and IT
support. He designed and implemented replacements for legacy SAS Grid environments
using cloud-native services on AWS and Azure.

The SAS Grid replacement architecture used containerized SAS 9.4 running on AWS
Elastic Kubernetes Service (EKS) and Azure Kubernetes Service (AKS), achieving
on-demand autoscaling based on workload rather than the fixed-capacity model of
traditional SAS Grid. This delivered significant infrastructure cost savings and
improved elasticity for pharma analytics workloads.

Mike also built custom SAS macros and optimized SAS and SQL code for pharmaceutical
clients. He has strong scripting skills in Bash, PowerShell, and Python.


PREVIOUS ROLE — SAS Institute, Director and Engineering Lead (2000 – 2018)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mike spent approximately 18 years at SAS Institute in progressively senior roles.

As Director (2013–2018), he led the development and launch of SAS Studio, the
web-based IDE for SAS. SAS Studio is a widely used product globally, delivered
as part of the core SAS platform. He led a team of 12–15 software engineers in
an Agile environment. He developed the XML framework for SAS Studio tasks, which
generates SAS and SQL code from a point-and-click interface. He integrated SAS
Studio with SAS Enterprise Guide and designed RESTful SAS microservices using
Java, Spring, and Spring Boot. He also worked on SAS Analytics Pro for AWS,
combining SAS Studio with EC2 deployments. He authored papers and presented at
SAS Global Forum.

As Senior Software Manager (2007–2013), he oversaw the development of SAS Retail
Space Management, integrating the MarketMax Space Planning product into the SAS
ecosystem. He managed 4–6 engineers and led the team's transition from waterfall
to Agile. He worked with large retail clients including Target, Walmart, and Publix
on complex data management and SQL optimization challenges.

As Software Engineer and Technical Lead (2000–2007), he pioneered SAS Digital
Marketing, a distributed server system for sending large volumes of customized
email. He led SAS's replacement of its external email marketing vendor with the
internally built SAS Digital Marketing product, saving $50,000 per year in
licensing fees. He developed several core SAS PROCs and datastep functions
including PROC HTTP, SOAP, PWENCODE, XSL, METADATA, and METACLEANSE. He worked
on core SAS user interfaces including DMS and SAS Enterprise Guide. His coding
experience at SAS includes C, Java, PHP, SAS Macro, and JavaScript with the
Dojo toolkit.
