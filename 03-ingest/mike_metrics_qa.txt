Mike Monaco — Key Metrics and Quantitative Impact (Hiring Manager FAQ)
======================================================================

These are the nine questions hiring managers and technical evaluators
most commonly ask about production cloud experience, with direct answers
from Mike's background.

QUESTION: How many AWS environments has Mike built or supported?
----------------------------------------------------------------
Mike has designed, built, and operated AWS environments across 25+
production SaaS tenants and 10+ bespoke cloud consulting engagements
for pharmaceutical and CRO clients under FDA-regulated requirements.

QUESTION: How many pharma clients has Mike worked with?
--------------------------------------------------------
Mike has delivered regulated cloud solutions for 20+ pharmaceutical and
CRO clients — ranging from boutique analytics shops to some of the
largest pharma and CRO organizations in the world. User populations
ranged from small pilot teams to enterprise rollouts of 2,000+ users.

QUESTION: What deployment time reduction has Mike achieved through automation?
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
In regulated pharmaceutical environments, the bottleneck in delivery is
never provisioning speed — it is validation: IQ/OQ sign-offs, change
control, and documentation. A time-to-provision number would be
misleading to anyone who knows GxP delivery.

What automation actually delivers in this context is standardization and
repeatability. Mike automated one-off client builds into validated,
reusable reference patterns using Terraform, CloudFormation, Packer,
GitHub Actions, and GitLab CI/CD — eliminating the rework that comes
from building each environment from scratch and reducing defects that
emerge from inconsistent manual processes. The value is quality and
consistency, not raw speed.

QUESTION: What cloud cost savings has Mike achieved?
-----------------------------------------------------
Mike built Lambda-based automation to power down idle SAS analytics
compute on weekends, reducing compute cost for those workloads by
approximately 30%. Additional cost optimization work included
right-sizing, storage architecture decisions (FSx tier selection,
lifecycle policies), and capacity planning for multi-client environments.

QUESTION: How many users has Mike supported?
---------------------------------------------
User populations ranged from small pilot teams to enterprise rollouts
supporting 2,000+ users across 20+ pharmaceutical and CRO clients.

QUESTION: What is Mike's data volume and storage footprint experience?
----------------------------------------------------------------------
Mike migrated hundreds of terabytes of clinical data from on-premises
Dell EMC Isilon storage to FSx for NetApp ONTAP in AWS using AWS
DataSync. He has designed and operated enterprise storage environments
using FSx for NetApp ONTAP, FSx for Lustre, AWS Storage Gateway, and
Nasuni across multiple large pharmaceutical organizations.

QUESTION: What are Mike's DR RTO and RPO targets?
-------------------------------------------------
Mike has designed and implemented cross-region warm-standby disaster
recovery architectures meeting documented SOP targets of 8-hour RTO
and 1-hour RPO. Actual RPO achieved via NetApp SnapMirror continuous
replication is approximately 15 minutes — significantly better than
the SOP requirement.

QUESTION: How many production incidents has Mike resolved?
----------------------------------------------------------
Mike serves as the senior AWS technical owner and escalation point
across client engineering, infrastructure, security, and application
teams — resolving complex production issues that are beyond the scope
of frontline support. He does not track a ticket count, and claiming
one would be misleading because his role is escalation and architecture,
not tier-1 resolution. The signal here is seniority: he is the person
other people escalate to.

QUESTION: How many reusable Terraform modules or reference architectures has Mike built?
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mike has built and published 100+ deployable reference architectures
across AWS, GCP, Azure, and OCI as open source on GitHub at
github.com/mamonaco1973. These are not internal modules — they are
publicly verifiable, production-quality implementations that anyone can
clone, deploy, and inspect. The count is checkable. The YouTube channel
(120,000 subscribers, 3.5 million views) walks through the majority of
them end to end.
