WHO IS MIKE MONACO?
-------------------
Michael Monaco is a senior cloud consultant, architect, and technical leader with
over 25 years of software engineering experience. He is currently Manager of Cloud
Consultants at Instem, a pharmaceutical life sciences company, where he leads a
team of 5 consultants delivering AWS and Azure cloud solutions for pharma clients.

He holds a BS in Computer Science from NC State University, a JD from North Carolina
Central University, and an AWS Certified Solutions Architect – Associate certification
earned in 2024.

LEADERSHIP STYLE
----------------
Mike describes his leadership style as "a colonel, not a general." He leads by doing —
staying technically hands-on and contributing meaningfully at the code and architecture
level regardless of whether he is in an individual contributor or team lead role. He has
managed teams of up to 15 engineers but always maintains deep technical involvement.
He is not interested in pure people management divorced from technical work.

CAREER SUMMARY
--------------

- 2 years at d-wise (2020–2022): pharma SAS Grid consulting, containerized SAS on EKS/AKS
- Present at Instem (2022–present): pharma cloud consulting, team lead, AWS/Azure migrations
- YouTube channel (2024–present): 120,000 subscribers, 3.5 million views

WHAT MIKE IS LOOKING FOR
------------------------
Mike is looking for a senior individual contributor or technical lead role in cloud
consulting or cloud architecture. He wants to remain hands-on technically — writing
Terraform, reviewing pull requests, designing architectures, contributing code —
while having the opportunity to lead and mentor when appropriate.

He prefers a consulting engagement model where he works across multiple clients and
projects, typically 6–18 months per engagement. The variety keeps him energized.
He is not willing to relocate but is open to remote, hybrid, or on-site arrangements.
