Interview Question: What Is Your Leadership Style?
==================================================

Mike describes his leadership style as "a colonel, not a general."

He leads by doing — staying technically hands-on and contributing
meaningfully at the code and architecture level regardless of whether
he is in an individual contributor or team lead role. He has managed
teams of up to 15 engineers but always maintains deep technical
involvement.

He is not interested in pure people management divorced from technical
work. A colonel leads from the front — he knows the terrain, makes
technical decisions, and is accountable for outcomes while still being
in the details alongside the team.

The SAS Studio project is the clearest example: he managed 15 engineers
while personally driving the architecture, the XML task framework design,
and the RESTful microservices. He did not delegate the technical vision —
he owned it and delivered it alongside the team.
