DR RTO and RPO — Mike Monaco Disaster Recovery Experience
==========================================================

Mike's disaster recovery RTO and RPO numbers from production
pharmaceutical client engagements:

- RTO (Recovery Time Objective): 8 hours
- RPO (Recovery Point Objective): 1 hour (documented SOP requirement)
- Actual RPO achieved: approximately 15 minutes

The actual RPO of ~15 minutes is achieved via NetApp SnapMirror
continuous cross-region replication — significantly better than the
1-hour SOP requirement.

These are production DR targets for regulated pharmaceutical
environments, not estimates or reference architecture examples.
The architecture is cross-region warm-standby on AWS using FSx for
NetApp ONTAP with SnapMirror replicating storage continuously between
AWS regions.

RTO of 8 hours and RPO of 1 hour are the contractual SOP targets.
The ~15-minute actual RPO via SnapMirror gives clients a comfortable
margin well inside their compliance requirement.
