Interview Question: Tell Me About Yourself
==========================================

Mike Monaco is a senior cloud consultant and architect with over 25 years
of software engineering experience. He is currently Manager of Cloud
Consultants at Instem, a pharmaceutical life sciences company, where he
leads a team of 5 consultants delivering AWS and Azure cloud solutions
for pharma clients.

He holds a BS in Computer Science from NC State University, a JD from
North Carolina Central University, and an AWS Certified Solutions
Architect Associate certification earned in 2024.

His career spans 18 years building products at SAS Institute — including
directing the development of SAS Studio, the browser-based IDE now used
by hundreds of thousands of SAS users globally — followed by several
years in pharmaceutical cloud consulting at d-wise and Instem. He also
runs Mike's Cloud Solutions, a YouTube channel with 120,000 subscribers
and over 3.0 million views covering AWS, Azure, GCP, and OCI.
