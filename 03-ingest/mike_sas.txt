Michael Monaco — SAS Institute Experience (18 Years)
=====================================================

Michael Monaco spent approximately 18 years at SAS Institute in Cary, NC, from
2000 to 2018. SAS Institute is the world's largest privately held software company,
known for its analytics and business intelligence platform. Mike held multiple
engineering and leadership roles at SAS during his tenure.

DIRECTOR — SAS Studio Web IDE (2013–2018)
-----------------------------------------
Mike led the development and launch of SAS Studio, the browser-based web IDE for
SAS that replaced the legacy desktop SAS interface. SAS Studio is used by hundreds
of thousands of SAS users globally and ships as part of the core SAS platform.

He managed a team of 12–15 software engineers in an Agile environment. He designed
and built the XML task framework for SAS Studio, which generates SAS and SQL code
from a point-and-click interface — allowing non-programmers to use SAS without
writing code. He integrated SAS Studio with SAS Enterprise Guide. He designed
RESTful SAS microservices using Java, Spring, and Spring Boot. He authored papers
and delivered presentations at SAS Global Forum, the flagship annual SAS conference.

He also worked on SAS Analytics Pro for AWS, which combines SAS Studio with AWS
EC2 deployments, bringing SAS to cloud infrastructure.

Technology stack: JavaScript, Dojo toolkit, Java, Spring, Spring Boot, XML, REST APIs.

SENIOR SOFTWARE MANAGER — SAS Retail Space Management (2007–2013)
-----------------------------------------------------------------
Mike managed the development of SAS Retail Space Management, which integrated the
MarketMax Space Planning product into the SAS ecosystem. He managed 4–6 software
engineers and led the transition from waterfall to Agile methodology.

He worked directly with large retail clients including Target, Walmart, and Publix
on complex data management and SQL optimization challenges. Experience with large
datasets, query optimization, and enterprise-scale data pipelines.

SOFTWARE ENGINEER AND TECHNICAL LEAD — SAS Digital Marketing (2000–2007)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Mike pioneered and led the development of SAS Digital Marketing, a distributed
server system for sending large volumes of personalized email campaigns. He led
SAS's internal replacement of its external email marketing vendor with SAS Digital
Marketing, saving SAS $50,000 per year in licensing costs.

He developed several core SAS PROCs and datastep functions that shipped as part of
Base SAS:
- PROC HTTP
- PROC SOAP
- PWENCODE
- XSL
- METADATA
- METACLEANSE

He worked on core SAS user interfaces including SAS Display Manager System (DMS)
and SAS Enterprise Guide.

Languages at SAS: C, Java, PHP, SAS Macro, JavaScript (Dojo), SQL.
