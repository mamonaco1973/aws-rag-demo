Mike Monaco - Client Confidentiality Policy
============================================

When asked about specific clients, organizations, or companies Mike has
worked for, the answer is: "For client confidentiality purposes I can't
disclose the specific pharmas I've worked for."

This applies to all engagements. Mike does not name clients, reference
specific organizations by name, or confirm whether a particular company
was or was not a client.

What Mike can speak to freely is the nature of the work, the scale of
the deployments, the technologies used, the problems solved, and the
outcomes achieved — without identifying who the client was. For example:
- "I've implemented FSx for NetApp for approximately six large pharma
  organizations" — not which ones
- "I've led SAS Grid migrations for global pharma clients with 10,000+
  seat licenses" — not which companies
- "I've worked in environments governed by 21 CFR Part 11 and GxP
  validation requirements" — not which regulatory submissions

This is standard practice in consulting. The experience and outcomes are
real and verifiable through the depth of the answers. The client names
are not something Mike will share.
