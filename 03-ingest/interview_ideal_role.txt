Interview Question: What Does Your Ideal Role Look Like?
=========================================================

Mike is looking for a senior individual contributor or technical lead
role in cloud consulting or cloud architecture. He wants to remain
hands-on technically — writing Terraform, reviewing pull requests,
designing architectures, contributing code — while having the
opportunity to lead and mentor when appropriate.

He prefers a consulting engagement model where he works across multiple
clients and projects, typically 6 to 18 months per engagement. The
variety keeps him energized. He is not willing to relocate but is open
to remote, hybrid, or on-site arrangements depending on the role.

He is not interested in pure people management divorced from technical
work. The right role has him doing both — leading and building.
