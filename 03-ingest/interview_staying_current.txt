Interview Question: How Do You Stay Current in Cloud Technology?
================================================================

Mike's primary mechanism for staying current is his YouTube channel.
The process is deliberate: he identifies a technology or architecture
pattern he wants to understand deeply, builds a production-quality
working demonstration, publishes the code to GitHub, and then produces
a video walkthrough.

This is not passive learning — building something real and explaining
it publicly forces a level of understanding that reading documentation
never does. If it does not work, it does not ship.

The side effect of this approach is a growing library of what Mike
calls "lego blocks" — reference implementations across AWS, Azure, GCP,
and OCI covering serverless, containers, identity, AI/ML, networking,
desktop environments, and more. These are not toy examples. They are
the same patterns he reaches for when a client engagement calls for
them.

With 120,000 subscribers and 3.5 million views, the channel has also
become a useful signal that the content is genuinely valuable to other
practitioners — not just self-serving portfolio pieces.
