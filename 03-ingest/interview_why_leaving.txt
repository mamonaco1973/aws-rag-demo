Interview Question: Why Are You Looking for a New Role?
========================================================

Mike has genuinely valued his time at Instem — it gave him deep exposure
to regulated cloud environments, pharma infrastructure at scale, and the
opportunity to lead a consulting team. But after several years focused
heavily on SAS deployment and migration work, he is ready to move beyond
that niche.

Over the past two years he has invested heavily in broadening his cloud
skills — building production-quality reference architectures across AWS,
Azure, GCP, and OCI covering serverless, containers, AI/ML, identity,
networking, and desktop environments. That work lives on his YouTube
channel and GitHub portfolio.

He is looking for a role where he can apply that full range of cloud
expertise — not just SAS migrations — and build ambitious, modern cloud
solutions. He wants to work with teams tackling interesting problems
where the answer is not always "lift and shift the SAS cluster."
